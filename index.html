About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in the vLAR Group at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, advised by Prof. Bo Yang since 2023.09. My research focuses on 3D vision, surface reconstruction, and 6D object pose estimation. I am interested in recovering continuous 3D geometry from visual observations, whether from unposed images, point clouds or Gaussian splats.

Previously, I received my M.Sc. degree in Computer Science and Technology from Xi'an Jiaotong University (2020.09 - 2023.06), advised by Prof. Yihong Gong, and my B.Sc. degree in Automation from Xi'an Jiaotong University (2016.09 - 2020.06).

Research

3D Surface Reconstruction

Ray-based and raylet distance fields that generalize across scenes, reconstructing surfaces from posed images, point clouds and Gaussian splats.

6D Object Pose Estimation

Symmetry-aware geometry correspondences for estimating the 6D pose of unseen objects, connecting point-cloud registration with least-squares pose fitting.

Anomaly Detection

Few-shot and weakly-supervised anomaly detection and segmentation for industrial inspection, including online and topology-preserving transfer settings.
